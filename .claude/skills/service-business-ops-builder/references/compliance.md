# Compliance checklist (flag for a human; not legal advice)

- SMS: written consent before marketing texts; opt-out handling (STOP);
  US carrier 10DLC business registration for app-sent texts (TCPA).
- Calls: California requires all-party consent to record — disclose at call start.
- AI disclosure: bots in chat/voice identify as automated (California has bot-disclosure rules).
- Collections: California's Rosenthal Act can apply to businesses collecting their own debts — keep reminders factual, no threats, no false urgency; escalations are human-only.
- Quotes/contracts: check the trade's rules (e.g., CA home-improvement contract requirements and CSLB license display for contractors).
- Data: store only what's needed; client customer data stays in the client's accounts except to process it; secrets in env vars.
- Record each check's result in clients/<client>/compliance.md with date.
