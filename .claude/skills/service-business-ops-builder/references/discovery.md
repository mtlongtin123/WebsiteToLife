# Discovery interview (plain language — owner is non-technical)

1. How do customers reach you today? (calls, texts, IG, Facebook, website, walk-ins) Roughly how many a week?
2. What happens when you miss a call? How fast do you usually reply?
3. What jobs do you want more of? What jobs do you turn down?
4. How do you price? Fixed prices, hourly, per square foot, site visit first?
5. What calendar and job tools do you use? (Google Calendar, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Square, paper…)
6. How do you invoice and get paid? How many invoices are late right now?
7. Who else works with you? Do you have backup people if someone can't show?
8. What should the AI NEVER say or promise?
9. What's the most annoying repetitive thing in your week?
10. What would make this worth it to you in 90 days?
